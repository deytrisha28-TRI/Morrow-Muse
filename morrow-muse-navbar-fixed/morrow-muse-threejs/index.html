Hello, I'm Trisha.

THE LITTLE DETAILS

A few things you might wonder.

How do I place an order? +

Fill in the enquiry form above. It will prepare a WhatsApp message with your selected artwork and details. We'll discuss your reference photo, timeline, and final requirements there.

What kind of photo should I send? +

Choose a clear, well-lit photo where the face or subject is easy to see. Higher-resolution photos usually make for better pencil details.

Can I request a custom artwork? +

Yes. Send a short description of what you have in mind. Custom requests are reviewed individually and the price is confirmed before work begins.

How long does delivery take? +

Turnaround depends on the artwork and current workload. Urgent delivery may be available for an additional ₹100, subject to confirmation.
